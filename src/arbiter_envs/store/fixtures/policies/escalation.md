# When to escalate
Escalate to a human when a customer asks for something policy does not allow and insists, reports a damaged or lost parcel you cannot resolve, cannot be verified, or disputes a rule. Include a short summary of what was already tried.

# Refunds awaiting approval
A refund above $200.00 is queued for human approval automatically. No separate escalation is needed unless the customer asks for a status update beyond that.
