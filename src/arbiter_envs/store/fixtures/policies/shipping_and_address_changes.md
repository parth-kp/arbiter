# Shipping times
Orders usually ship within two business days of being placed. Tracking is available once an order has shipped. A carrier delay does not change the refund or return rules; offer to escalate if the delay is long.

# Address changes
The shipping address can only be changed while an order is placed or processing. Once an order has shipped, been delivered or been cancelled, the address cannot be changed. For shipped orders the customer should contact the carrier to redirect the parcel.
